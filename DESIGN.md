# Interface direction

The calculator interface adapts patterns observed on the public [Greenpixie homepage](https://greenpixie.com/) and [Greenpixie AI Energy Calculator](https://greenpixie.com/ai-energy-calculator): a centered, high-contrast heading; a pale mint input surface beside a white impact-results surface; numbered, progressive input sections; rounded selection controls; and compact energy-attribution summaries.

This is a visual reference, not a Greenpixie product or endorsement. The HPC Cost Lab name, creator attribution, calculator content, calculations, and CSS mark are specific to this research tool. No Greenpixie logo, image, customer mark, or page screenshot is embedded.
