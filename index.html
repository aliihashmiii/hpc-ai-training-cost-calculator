FOCUS 1.4 Cost & Usage CSVStandard column names; estimate values are kept in x_ extension columns.
Important: this is not an invoice dataset

The CSV uses the FOCUS 1.4 Cost and Usage column set. This calculator has no invoice amount, billing account ID, invoice issuer, provider names, SKU price ID, or billing periods. It leaves those billing facts blank, keeps standardized billing costs at zero, and writes the modeled electricity estimate to an x_ custom column. The result is an estimate-oriented FOCUS 1.4 scaffold, not a complete or validator-conformant billing dataset.

HOW IT'S CALCULATED
1 kWh = 3,600,000 J. Estimated electricity cost = IT energy × PUE × tariff. Energy entries are measured or explicitly entered; runtime is never used as a proxy. Sample values are illustrative.
